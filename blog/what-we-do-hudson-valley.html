Junk Removal & Moving Services in the Hudson Valley: What We Do

A rundown of everything Junk & Move Pros handles across the Hudson Valley.

Junk Removal & Cleanouts

Moving Services

Local moves, furniture delivery, and disassembly and assembly. We bring our own dollies, blankets, and straps — and we're upfront about the price before the truck leaves the yard.
