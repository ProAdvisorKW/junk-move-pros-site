Before You Call: How to Get an Accurate Junk Removal Quote With Just a Few Photos

One of the fastest ways to get an accurate price on a junk removal or cleanout job is also one of the easiest: send us a few pictures. Here's why it matters and how to get the most useful shots before you call or text.

Why Photos Speed Up Your Quote

A short phone call is a good start, but photos let us see the actual volume, the stairs or doorways we'll be working around, and whether anything needs special handling — an old CRT TV, a mattress, a swing set, or an appliance with refrigerant. That means the price we give you over text or email is much closer to the price on pickup day, with fewer surprises for either of us.

What to Include in Your Photos

A wide shot of the full room, garage, shed, or pile so we can gauge total volume. Close-ups of anything heavy, awkward, or hazardous — pianos, safes, appliances, tires, paint cans. Access details — any stairs, narrow hallways, or tight parking that could affect the job. And your general area or address so we can estimate drive time and disposal fees accurately.

What Happens After You Send Photos

We'll text or call back, usually within a few hours, with a firm price based on what you sent. If the job matches the photos, that's the price you pay — no surprise fees when the truck shows up. Same-day and next-day appointments are available for most jobs across the Hudson Valley.

Ready to get a real price? Send a few photos, your city, and zip code to (845) 402-0555, or reach out through our contact page — we'll get you a firm quote fast.
