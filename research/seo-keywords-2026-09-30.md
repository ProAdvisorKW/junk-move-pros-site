## 1. Top findings (read this first)

1. **The franchises barely contest Hudson Valley town-level SERPs.** "Junk removal Poughkeepsie NY" returns only small local haulers (Junk Pros NY, Neil's All Junk Hauled) — no 1-800-GOT-JUNK, no College Hunks. "Junk removal Ellenville/Wurtsboro/Monticello" (Anthony's home turf) returns only tiny operators with weak websites. **The local map pack + town pages are winnable in months 1–6.**
2. **"Estate cleanout Hudson Valley" is a vacuum.** Results skew to Albany and Connecticut — no dominant mid-Hudson Valley (Orange/Dutchess/Ulster) estate-cleanout player. This is Junk & Move Pros' highest-value gap: estate jobs are large tickets and the exact audience of the 818-agent realtor campaign.
3. **Moving keywords are owned by aggregators, not local movers.** "Movers Kingston NY" = moveBuddha, HireAHelper, U-Haul. No local full-service mover ranks organically with transparent pricing. A local "how much do movers cost in [town]" page with real Hudson Valley numbers can out-localize them.
4. **LoadUp's playbook is the template to copy:** programmatic `/{town}/{service}/` pages (e.g. goloadup.com/poughkeepsie/mattress-removal/) with FAQ schema and "typical cost in [town]" blocks. They rank #1 for "mattress removal Hudson Valley." Junk & Move Pros can run the same playbook as a *real local crew* — and beat LoadUp's marketplace positioning on the "owner answers the phone" angle.
5. **Pricing content is the trust weapon and the traffic weapon.** Angi's programmatic "how much does junk removal cost in {city}" pages dominate cost queries, but they're generic national data. A Hudson Valley-specific cost guide using real local numbers (Blue Collar's published ranges prove this ranks) targets the #1 customer anxiety — quote-to-bill inflation — which is also the #1 franchise complaint in reviews.

## 5. Local SEO quick wins

### Service-area page structure (copy the Junkluggers/LoadUp architecture)

```
/service-areas/                      → hub: county list + map + "why local" copy
/junk-removal-{town}-ny/             → one page per priority town (start with 8–10)
/estate-cleanouts-hudson-valley/     → flagship money page (the vacuum keyword)
/moving-help-{town}-ny/              → moving pages for top towns
/services/{service}/                 → service pages: furniture-removal, appliance-removal,
                                       mattress-removal, hoarding-cleanout, storage-unit-cleanout,
                                       curbside-pickup, yard-debris-removal, commercial-junk-removal,
                                       hot-tub-removal, shed-removal
```

**Rules so the pages rank instead of getting filtered as thin content:**
- Every town page gets 400+ unique words: town-specific landmarks, neighborhoods served, transfer-station/dump notes, parking/access quirks — never swap only the town name.
- FAQ schema on every page (steal Junkluggers' question shapes: "Do you offer same-day junk removal in {town}?", "How much does junk removal cost in {town}?", "Do you offer curbside pickup?").
- Internal linking: hub ↔ town pages ↔ service pages ↔ relevant blog posts.
- Priority towns (months 1–3): Ellenville, Kingston, Poughkeepsie, Newburgh, Middletown, New Paltz, Monticello, Wurtsboro, Port Jervis, Beacon.
- Each page: click-to-call (845) 402-0555 above the fold, service-area list, 2–3 local FAQs, one embedded job photo when available.

### Google Business Profile (do this week — it's the #1 local ranking factor)

- **Primary category:** `Junk removal service`
- **Secondary categories (up to 9):** `Moving company`, `Mover`, `Waste management service`, `Hauling service`, `Garbage collection service`, `Furniture removal service` (if available), `Recycling center` (only if accurate)
- **Service areas:** list Ulster, Orange, Dutchess, Sullivan counties + named towns (GBP service-area businesses don't show an address — correct setup for a truck-based operator).
- **Services list:** mirror the website services with descriptions; add "Estate cleanouts," "Furniture removal," "Appliance removal," "Mattress removal," "Moving help," "Hoarding cleanup," "Storage unit cleanout."
- **Posts:** weekly GBP post = the week's blog post or job photo (free, shows in the knowledge panel).
- **Q&A:** seed 5–6 real questions/answers yourself ("Do you give the price before you start?", "Do you do same-day pickup in Kingston?").

### Review strategy basics

- **Ask after every job**, same day, via SMS with the direct GBP review link. Strike while the truck is still warm.
- Benchmark: Junkluggers Clarkstown sits at **4.9★ / 170 reviews** — that's the number to chase in 12 months. Target: 10 reviews in month 1, 50 by month 6.
- **Respond to every review** within 48 hours (owner voice). Review responses are indexed and build keyword relevance.
- Never buy, incentivize, or gate reviews (against Google policy — one suspension wipes the map-pack presence).
- Put a QR code to the review link on the truck, the invoice, and the quote follow-up text.
- Mine reviews for keywords: when customers write "estate cleanout in Newburgh," that exact phrase in reviews reinforces rankings.
